Safety · accountability · reform

Kappa Delta Epsilon

As CORE Risk Manager, I coordinate with Dartmouth Safety and Security, Hanover Police, the Town of Hanover, the Office of the President, and campus services to plan safer events and contribute to Greek life reform. The work means translating policy, logistics, and community needs into practices people can follow.

President · previously Outreach Chair

Sexual Assault Peer Alliance

I support peers affected by gender-based violence and other campus concerns, and help lead prevention and survivor-support work across campus. After serving as Outreach Chair, I now serve as president and coordinate with Dartmouth’s Title IX office, the Counseling Center and medical services at Dick’s House, the Student Wellness Center, and other student organizations.

SAPA members complete extensive training to provide informed, empathic, empowerment-based peer support. The work asks for discretion, careful listening, and a clear understanding of the resources available to each person.

Mentor · since arriving at Dartmouth

DREAM mentorship

I have mentored with DREAM since I first arrived in Hanover, building sustained relationships with young people from affordable-housing communities in the Upper Valley. Showing up over time—through shared activities, group time, and one-on-one support—has given me perspective beyond campus and made community a lived commitment rather than an abstract idea.

Peer support

Teen Lifeline

Teen Lifeline is a confidential peer-to-peer crisis hotline for teens. As a trained Peer Counselor, I supported more than 1,000 young people, including callers experiencing suicidal thoughts and acute crises. I helped save lives. The work taught me how to stay calm under pressure, listen for what matters, and act when the stakes are life and death.

2021–2022

Girl Up Coalition Leader for Arizona

Girl Up named me a Coalition Leader for Arizona in its 2021–2022 class. Girl Up describes Coalition Leaders as young people who unite clubs and communities in the movement for global gender equality, supporting advocacy and fundraising, mentoring peers, and coordinating collaborative work.

Earlier work · BLOOM365

Teen dating violence prevention with BLOOM365

As a teenage peer advocate for BLOOM365, I worked on teen dating violence prevention: peer advocacy, risk factors, and red flags in abusive relationships. In March 2023 I joined BLOOM365 founder and CEO Donna Bartos for an Arizona Horizon interview on Arizona PBS about that work.

In 2023 I also spoke at the NOVA49 conference closing ceremony in New Orleans through BLOOM365. Donna Bartos identified me as the founder of its Gen Z Advisory Board and summarized my call to action: make space for teens where decisions about their futures are made.
